FAQ

Is patient data safe?

Boli takes consent in the first message, collects only what a booking needs, and is designed around India's DPDP Act principles.

Does the AI give medical advice?

No. Medical questions are passed to the doctor. The bot handles timings, fees, location and bookings.

Do I need new software?

No. It works on your WhatsApp Business number and your Google Calendar.

Will it support voice calls?

Yes, planned after WhatsApp is stable. Voice needs a telephony provider and Indic speech models.
